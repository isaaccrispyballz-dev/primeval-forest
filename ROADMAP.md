# Primeval: full feature and item list

Primeval is a survival game in the same genre as The Forest, but with its own world, story, creatures and items. Each line below becomes one piece of work: a proper model, its animations and sounds, and the gameplay behind it, delivered as a test APK.

`[x]` done · `[~]` started, needs the full-quality pass · `[ ]` not started

## 1. Core feel (always on)
- [x] Hand-push arm movement (plant hands on ground, trunks, logs and push, launch on release, climb trunks)
- [x] Stick walking and snap turning
- [ ] Comfort settings: smooth turn, turn speed, vignette when moving fast, seated mode, left-handed mode
- [ ] Full-body shadow and visible forearms/sleeves
- [ ] Swimming and wading (paddle with your arms)
- [ ] Ledge grabbing and climbing rock faces with the same hand-push system
