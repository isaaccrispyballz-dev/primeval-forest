Lanternwood

Your survey drone went down somewhere in an old-growth valley that isn't on any map. Night here is long, and the things that live in the dark don't like fire.

Quest 3: press Enter VR. Left stick moves, right stick snap-turns. Swing the axe (right hand) into a trunk to chop. A builds a campfire for 5 wood.

Desktop: click to play, WASD to move, mouse to look, click to chop, F for campfire.
